# Combined Scope and bounded Swing carry

A new PAPER owner can explicitly enroll Degen, fixed Scope and bounded Swing carry
in one shared coordinator/runtime. Each independent research account still owns its
own cash, reservations, fills, model budget and loss controls. This connects native
fixture plumbing; authentic provider/model, overnight and economic acceptance remain
open. Existing frozen populations cannot acquire the new configuration.

## Explicit native contracts

`scope_carry="shared-scope-carry-v1"` must match on `OptionExecutionPolicy` and
`SharedScopePolicy`. All three families and their loss policies are required.
The Swing policy must separately enroll its existing bounded carry contract, exact
session limits, original target, catalyst coverage and unfilled lifecycle. Scope
keeps its existing native watch/arming/admission and fixed-plan contract.

Model-off shared selection is supported. Full entry reasoning additionally requires
the marker on `EntryReasoningPolicy`, `scope-fixed-plan-v1` and an explicit carry
horizon limit. Its new `shared-scope-carry-plan-v1` schema permits a multi-session
horizon only when native review proves that the selected Swing candidate has original
carry terms. Degen retains its ordinary intraday cap. A Scope selection still leaves
all geometry and horizon fields null and selects the exact supplied fixed plan.
The new hashed entry prompt includes both family contracts. Scope still requires
fresh owned admission after settling the model charge, with original source clocks,
revision, quantity/price ceilings and approval deadline.

Optional management requires the same marker on `PositionReasoningPolicy`, plus
both existing Scope management and Swing carry policies. Its separately hashed
combined prompt describes both contexts. The packet contains only the context of
the actual owned holding: Scope's original fixed plan and paid entry thesis, or
Swing's original locked daily scan and enumerated carry sessions. It cannot use a
Scope context as carry evidence or give a Scope holding an overnight horizon.
MAINTAIN, TIGHTEN and EXIT_ALL retain native protection and actual fill authority.

The complete enrollment is checked before coordinator creation, by runtime settings,
and again when reconstructing stored shared evidence/export. Management packets
bind the marker to the execution owner. Prompt/schema hashes, original native
selection, paid receipts and model charges remain part of stored validation.
All new optional markers are omitted when absent; legacy prompts and serialization
retain their historical values.

## Carry, watches and protection

One carried Swing holding reserves the account and blocks competing entries. Its
entry approval stays short lived and its DAY order still expires in the original
entry session. Original carry exit, first target, premium/underlying protection,
partial-fill ownership and final whole-position close remain native. Overnight
coverage is explicitly `NOT_CONTINUOUSLY_OBSERVED`; reopening quotes never prove
an overnight price path or a guaranteed stop fill.

The existing continuous Scope transport binds this new owner without changing its
packet versions or durable sequence/action protocol. Unfilled Scope watches still
expire at their original session cutoff, including when a Swing position remains
held or the feed disappears. A missing feed fences new entries but does not erase
latched native exits. Restart reconstructs the exact original holding and deadline;
fresh exact-contract quotes are still required to dispatch a close.

Scope holdings retain their original same-session targets, premium profile and
protection deadline. Management cannot postpone a Scope deadline because the owner
also permits Swing carry. Independent mandatory exits keep precedence over paid
advice in either family.

## Accounting and boundaries

Portable native Scope export includes all three families and validates complete
combined enrollment. Stored held protection now also compares original carry terms
against both the native ledger and venue snapshot. Every entry/management charge,
execution fee and explicit data/host allocation is included. Operating allocations
must cover the supplied entire holding period, including overnight carry. Missing
costs or unresolved positions keep net results unknown; no overnight mark, return,
drawdown, comparison result or operating expense is inferred.

Scope tape/model/comparison adapters reject combined carry. Neither the old replay
adapter nor choice-only B can be attached to this new owner. The separate
[Scope series contract](OPTIONS_SCOPE_SERIES.md) now retains continuous independent
accounts for session-close tapes. Combined carry series still requires full model
enrollment and overnight comparison; this native enrollment does not broaden old tapes.
Remaining family completion and mixed stock/options ownership also remain open.
UI and authentic PAPER acceptance follow the remaining plumbing.
