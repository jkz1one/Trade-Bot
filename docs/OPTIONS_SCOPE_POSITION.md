# Shared Scope position reasoning

New PAPER populations may freeze
`PositionReasoningPolicy(scope_management="scope-fixed-position-v1", max_lease_seconds=...)`
alongside the shared Scope entry AI configuration. Both sides must be explicitly
enrolled; a legacy entry-only or deterministic cohort cannot gain management on
reopen. The same bounded worker and single native account support Degen, session-
close Swing and Scope. Combined Scope/overnight carry remains rejected.

For a held Scope contract, the supplied `DegenFrame.scope` must identify the exact
consumed play and held SPXW instrument. Its completed SPX/SPY/QQQ histories and
current typed context are recomputed through the frozen Scope context policy.
Missing, stale, mismatched or incomplete coverage prevents a paid management call.
A new entry pattern is not required to review an existing position. New pattern
observations cannot replace the original trade geometry.

The packet retains the verified native entry, its fixed Scope admission and plan,
current owned position, account, executable quote, underlying and completed Scope
scan. The verified paid entry selection retains its original AI thesis and
uncertainty alongside the deterministically materialized native plan. It preserves original quantity, invalidation, both target milestones,
allocations, premium profile and time protection. Current remaining quantity,
target progress and tighter protection come from native position state. Original
entry evidence remains historical; it is never presented as a current admission.
The complete paid entry and its distinct native source key are checked before a
management attempt, application, dispatch and stored attribution.

The opt-in prompt permits only the existing MAINTAIN, TIGHTEN and EXIT_ALL schema.
No quantity, replacement target, deadline extension, new contract or partial exit
is available to the model. Scope uses its own completed scan as the mandatory
context reference; ordinary Degen/Swing packets keep their existing context.
Prompt/model/rates, bounds and policy are frozen with the new owner.

Independent supervision runs before and after the model. Original slot/lease,
position revision, full settled usage and fresh account/quote checks still apply.
Mandatory stops, target actions, premium and time protection take precedence.
Known charges survive late/rejected advice; missing usage remains unknown and
halts further model admission. No callback or interrupted slot retries a call.
Stop changes and full-close preparation use the existing atomic consumption path.
Partial target exits retain the same entry and cost ledger.

Stored management attribution and native portable export reconstruct the Scope
entry link, proposal, application and actual fills. Closed outcomes include every
entry and management charge and every execution fee. Independent owners retain
separate capital, reservations and loss counters. Missing costs or unfinished
exposure do not become a zero-cost or complete result.

This is offline fixture plumbing. Common Scope A/B/C/D tapes, continuous watch
runtime input, remaining family/mixed ownership work and final UI/authentic-data
acceptance remain separate. No provider/model/broker call, deployment or LIVE
activation is part of this slice. See [status](SLICE2_STATUS.md) for installed proof.
